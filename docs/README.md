All requirements are bound to an immutable prefix to ensure seamless end-to-end verification:

* `BR-xxx`: Business Requirements
* `CR-xxx`: Customer Requirements
* `BA-xxx`: Business Assumptions
* `BD-xxx`: Owner Approved Business Decisions
* `CAP-xxx`: Studio Capabilities
* `PR-xxx`: Product Requirements
* `UX-xxx`: User Experience Requirements
* `FR-xxx`: Functional System Requirements
* `NFR-xxx`: Non-Functional & Performance Requirements
* `AI-xxx`: AI Engine & Model Specifications
* `SEC-xxx`: Security, Privacy, and Threat Defenses
* `QA-xxx`: Quality Assurance & Verification Test Cases
* `OPS-xxx`: Operational Procedures & Human Policies
