## 4. Voice & Linguistic Standards Summary

When authoring copy for `[STUDIO_NAME]`, follow these core voice attributes:

1. **Be Intelligent & Systems-Minded**: Talk about architectural integrity, data models, state machines, and unit economics.
2. **Be Unambiguously Clear**: Use simple, precise business language. Explain technical decisions through their commercial impact.
3. **Be Confident & Candid**: Present tradeoffs openly. Never hide engineering limitations or commercial uncertainties.
4. **Be Economical with Words**: Prefer short, punchy, active sentences over sprawling passive paragraphs.
5. **Never Use Unverified AI Hype**: Strictly ban phrases like *"disruptive revolutionary AI"*, *"magical autonomous software"*, or *"100% automated enterprise"*.

---

## 5. Visual Principles Summary (Conceptual Direction)

* **Aesthetic Tone**: Precision Systems Atelier—clean, technical, structured, and human-centered.
* **Negative Space**: Generous whitespace around all structural cards and reading text to maintain high cognitive clarity.
* **Typography Roles**: Geometric or neo-grotesque sans-serif for headings and UI; monospace for technical schematics and code.
* **Motion Window**: Snappy, purposeful micro-animations between **150ms and 250ms**. Zero frivolous, slow, or bouncing motion.
* **AI Depiction**: Abstract architectural diagrams, structured data flows, and clean token pulses. Never metallic robot hands or glowing brains.

## 7. Mandatory Claim Governance & Truth in Advertising

> [!CAUTION]
> ### Inviolable Rule on Marketing Claims & Evidence
> **No unsupported metric, performance guarantee, client result, market claim, or commercial promise may appear as factual marketing copy without verified empirical evidence and explicit Principal Architect sign-off.**  
> *(Traceability: `BD-006`, `BD-013`, `BD-014`, `BR-GDL-001`)*

### 7.1 Specific Claim Governance Rules

1. **No Fabricated Metrics**:
   - Do NOT claim *"Boosts conversion by 300%"* or *"Cuts costs by 80%"* unless certified by an audited, signed client case study.
   - Use objective, qualitative phrasing instead: *"Significantly reduces manual data entry overhead"* or *"Compresses time-to-market by assembling modular, pre-tested components"*.
2. **Estimation Disclaimers (`BD-006`)**:
   - Every algorithmic price or timeline range displayed by the AI Discovery Engine must feature the standard non-binding disclaimer:
     > *"Estimates are indicative planning ranges generated for scope qualification. They do NOT constitute binding commercial quotations. Binding SOWs require human Principal Architect review."*
3. **No Contractual SLA Claims (`BD-013`)**:
   - Internal response targets (e.g. 24 business hours for proposal reviews) must be presented as target operating standards (*"Typically reviewed within 1 business day"*), NEVER as legally binding contractual SLAs with financial remedies.
4. **Compliance Language Integrity (`BD-014`)**:
   - Never claim formal statutory certifications (e.g. *"SOC-2 Type II Certified"*, *"HIPAA Guaranteed"*, *"ISO 27001 Certified"*) without independent audit certificates.
   - Describe systems as *"compliance-ready, engineered adhering to strict data privacy and security best practices"*.

## 8. Content Quality & Publishing Checklist

Before any web page, marketing document, social post, or proposal is published under the `[STUDIO_NAME]` brand, it must be verified against this 7-point checklist:

- [ ] **1. Placeholder Check**: Is `[STUDIO_NAME]` strictly used without unapproved brand variations?
- [ ] **2. Core Philosophy**: Does the piece embody *"Technology should adapt to the business"*?
- [ ] **3. AI Duality Enforced**: Is AI framed as leverage and humans as judgement?
- [ ] **4. Zero False Precision**: Are all pricing and timeline figures presented as indicative ranges?
- [ ] **5. Metric Audit**: Are there any unvalidated percentages, revenue numbers, or ROI guarantees? (If yes, eliminate or rephrase as qualitative objectives).
- [ ] **6. IP Ownership Affirmed**: Is 100% client code ownership clearly stated?
- [ ] **7. Primary CTA Alignment**: Does the piece direct prospects to *"Start With Your Problem"*?
