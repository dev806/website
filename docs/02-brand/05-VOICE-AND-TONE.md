## 1. Executive Summary & Voice Principles

How `[STUDIO_NAME]` speaks is as important as what we build. In an industry overrun by inflated hype, technical obfuscation, and pseudo-scientific AI claims, our linguistic identity is a key competitive differentiator.

We speak with the quiet authority of seasoned engineers and thoughtful business analysts. We treat our clients as intellectual equals—respecting their domain expertise while guiding them with clarity, empathy, and technical rigor.

## 3. Vocabulary & Sentence Style Standards

### 3.1 Sentence Structure & Rhythm
* **Keep Sentences Concise**: Prefer active, direct subject-verb-object constructions. Avoid sprawling, multi-clause passive academic prose.
* **Vary Sentence Length**: Combine short, punchy statements with measured, explanatory sentences to create natural reading cadence.
* **Economize Words**: If an adjective does not add information, cut it. Every word must earn its place on the screen.

### 3.2 Curated Lexicon (Preferred Terms vs. Avoided Terms)

| Preferred Terminology | Avoided / Prohibited Terms | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Technology Studio" / "Systems Partner"** | "Software Agency" / "Dev Shop" | We build proprietary assets and partner long-term; we are not a coding commodity sweatshop. |
| **"AI-Native Leverage"** | "Autonomous AI Magic" / "AI Miracle" | We use AI as a precise cognitive multiplier governed by humans, not an unsupervised deity. |
| **"Proprietary Assets" / "Owned Code"** | "Rented Software" / "Licensed Scripts" | We celebrate client equity and total IP independence from vendor lock-in. |
| **"Operational Friction" / "Bottlenecks"** | "Digital Transformation" (cliché) | Concrete, P&L-focused terms resonate with operators; buzzwords trigger skepticism. |
| **"Solution Blueprint" / "Architecture"** | "Feature Checklist" / "Deliverables List" | Emphasizes holistic systems design rather than disjointed task lists. |
| **"Indicative Planning Range"** | "AI Quote" / "Instant Estimate" | Reinforces that numbers are non-binding planning aids requiring human SOW review (`BD-006`). |
| **"Durable Software"** | "Future-Proof" (overused cliché) | Nothing is immortal in tech; software is made durable through clean architecture and tests. |

## 4. Communication Rules by Domain

### 4.1 Technical Language Rules
* **Explain the "Why", Not Just the "What"**: When discussing technology (e.g. Python, FastAPI, relational schema isolation), immediately connect the architectural choice to business maintainability, speed, or cost efficiency.
* **Be Specific, Never Hand-Wave**: Speak in concrete terms: write *"asynchronous non-blocking I/O"* instead of *"super-fast performance"*; write *"relational snapshot isolation"* instead of *"advanced database tech"*.
* **No Elitism**: Technical competence is demonstrated by making complex concepts simple, not by using obscure jargon to confuse the client.

### 4.2 Business Language Rules
* **Speak the Language of the Balance Sheet**: Frame technical recommendations around gross margins, customer acquisition velocity, transcription error rates, employee churn, and operational capacity.
* **Acknowledge the Client's Domain Superiority**: The client knows their logistics business, medical practice, or retail supply chain better than we do. Our job is to listen, translate, and architect—not lecture them on their industry.

### 4.3 AI Language Rules (`BD-010`, `BD-014`)
* **The Non-Negotiable Rule**: Always communicate AI capabilities within the boundary of our guiding doctrine:
  > **"AI handles leverage. Humans handle judgement."**
* **Never Personify AI**: Do not refer to AI as "thinking", "deciding", "feeling", or "inventing". Use active tool verbs: *"the model synthesizes", "the engine classifies", "the algorithm extracts"*.
* **Never Promise Flawless Autonomy**: Be completely candid about hallucination risks, edge-case limitations, and why senior human architects review every output before production deployment.
* **Privacy & Security Realism**: Describe privacy through concrete engineering controls (zero-data-retention commercial API terms, client-side PII regex scrubbers) rather than vague marketing promises like *"100% impenetrable military-grade security"*.

## 6. Prohibited Practices & Linguistic Banned List

To protect brand integrity, the following practices are strictly prohibited across all published content:

1. **Zero Invented Statistics**: Never write *"97% of businesses fail without AI"* or *"our clients see a 4.8x ROI"* unless certified by empirical, audited case study data.
2. **Zero Unsupported Performance Guarantees**: Never promise *"sub-500ms latency worldwide"* or *"99.999% guaranteed uptime"* without contractual architecture backing.
3. **Zero Fake Urgency / Dark Patterns**: Never use artificial scarcity countdown timers (*"Only 2 discovery spots left this week!"*) or manipulative subscription traps.
4. **Zero Competitor Slander**: We never insult named competitors or demean other agencies. We simply highlight our distinct structural advantages (upfront discovery, reusable technology, 100% IP ownership).

## 7. Human Communication Principles

When our team interacts with clients directly (over email, video conference, or shared channels), we abide by four relationship standards:

* **Candor Over People-Pleasing**: If a client requests a feature that is commercially unviable, technologically brittle, or unnecessarily complex, we tell them immediately and explain why. True partners protect clients from expensive mistakes.
* **Accountability for Glitches**: When bugs or edge cases occur, we do not deflect, blame third-party APIs, or make excuses. We diagnose the root cause, transparently communicate the remediation plan, and ship the fix.
* **Generosity with Insight**: In our free diagnostics and initial blueprints, we share genuine architectural wisdom. We do not withhold basic answers to hold clients hostage.
* **Calm Reliability**: When client business stakes are high, we remain the calm, steady technical anchor in the room.
