| **`WEB-REQ-019`** | **Screen Reader Streaming Feedback (`aria-live`)**: Dynamic HTMX updates during AI synthesis must announce progress via polite ARIA live regions. | Keeps visually impaired users informed during asynchronous processing. | **Must Have** | **MVP** | `UX-003`, `PRD-REQ-003` |
