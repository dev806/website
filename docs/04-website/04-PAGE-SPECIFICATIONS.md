## 2. Homepage Specification (`/`)

### 1. Page Objective
Establish `[STUDIO_NAME]` as a premium AI-native Technology Studio; validate the core philosophy that *"Technology should adapt to the business — not the business to technology"*; drive qualified visitors directly into the **AI Project Discovery** engine.

### 2. Target Audience
Founders of venture-backed startups, owner-operators of growing SMEs, and innovation/technical leaders seeking durable engineering and automation (`BD-002`).

### 3. User Intent
Evaluating whether `[STUDIO_NAME]` understands their operational problem, possesses genuine architectural capability, and provides a low-risk, high-clarity engagement model.

### 4. Primary Call-to-Action
**"Start With Your Problem"** (Deep purple/indigo button, links to `/discovery`).

### 5. Secondary Call-to-Action
**"Explore What We Build"** (Subtle bordered ghost button, smooth-scrolls to Section 4 / links to `/services`).

#### Section 1: Hero
* **Headline**: *"Have a business problem? Let's turn it into technology."*
* **Sub-headline**: *"We build custom software, deploy applied AI, automate repetitive workflows, and integrate fragmented systems for startups, SMEs, and growing businesses."*
* **Supporting Reassurance**: *"You don't need to know what technology you need. Start with what's broken, manual, or slowing you down."*
* **Interactive Micro-Element**: Low-friction natural language input preview box with button: **"Start With Your Problem"**.
* **Visual Direction**: Dark obsidian aesthetic, deep radial indigo ambient glow, calm typographic layout, zero chaotic 3D animations.

#### Section 2: Problem Recognition (Business Language First)
* **Section Heading**: *"Software shouldn't be built from assumptions. It should be built from problems."*
* **Core Content**: 8 interactive problem cards highlighting acute operational bottlenecks:
  1. *Manual Operations*: Repetitive human copy-pasting across spreadsheets.
  2. *Disconnected Tools*: CRM, billing, and inventory living in unintegrated silos.
  3. *Outdated Systems*: Legacy software that breaks whenever business volume increases.
  4. *Data Invisibility*: Decisions made on intuition because reporting requires 3 days of manual prep.
  5. *Scaling Ceilings*: Customer inquiries piling up faster than hiring can solve.
  6. *Unrealized AI Potential*: Wanting to leverage modern AI models without hallucination risks or data leakage.
  7. *Vendor Lock-in & Bloat*: Paying massive SaaS subscriptions for tools that only solve 20% of your workflow.
  8. *Technical Debt*: MVPs duct-taped together that now threaten core stability.
* **Governance Rule**: Zero fabricated percentages, fake market stats, or unsupported claims (`BR-GDL-003`).

#### Section 3: The Translation Layer
* **Section Heading**: *"The missing step in modern technology isn't code. It's translation."*
* **Conceptual Graphic**: A structured horizontal architectural diagram:
  $$\text{Business Problem} \longrightarrow \text{Cognitive Translation} \longrightarrow \text{Systems Blueprint} \longrightarrow \text{Durable Code}$$
* **Body Copy**: *"Most agencies ask what features you want to build. We ask what business problem you need to solve. Technology should adapt to your operational reality—never the other way around."*

#### Section 4: What We Build (5 Capability Pillars)
* **Section Heading**: *"Full-Stack Engineering. Applied Intelligence. Seamless Automation."*
* **Pillar Cards**:
  1. **Custom Software**: High-performance web applications, internal operational tooling, customer portals.
  2. **Applied AI**: Enterprise LLM integrations, deterministic extraction pipelines, automated document cognition.
  3. **Workflow Automation**: Event-driven task automation, background job queues, human-in-the-loop triage.
  4. **API & Systems Integration**: Enterprise ERP/CRM bridges, webhook infrastructure, data synchronization.
  5. **Scale & Modernization**: Database optimization, legacy refactoring, performance engineering.
* **CTA on each card**: Contextual link to `/services/[pillar]` and `/discovery?pillar=[pillar]`.

#### Section 5: The AI Discovery Spotlight (Product-Led Core)
* **Section Heading**: *"Explore your solution before writing a single line of code."*
* **Interactive Preview**: A visual representation of the 7-stage Discovery Stepper:
  - Natural Problem Input $\rightarrow$ Clarification Questions $\rightarrow$ Opportunity Map $\rightarrow$ Indicative Sizing Bands.
* **Primary Reassurance**: *"Free. Instantaneous. 100% ungated initial exploration."*
* **Direct Button**: **"Start With Your Problem"** (`/discovery`).

#### Section 6: How We Work (The 5-Point North Star)
* **Section Heading**: *"A disciplined pathway from friction to working software."*
* **5-Stage Flow**:
  1. *Understand the business* (Deep operational context).
  2. *Translate the problem* (Mapping friction to technical patterns).
  3. *Build what matters* (Clean, modular, high-craft engineering).
  4. *Automate what shouldn't be manual* (Event pipelines and tasks).
  5. *Scale what works* (Refactoring, performance tuning, and stability).
* **The Collaboration Doctrine**: Prominently features the law: *"AI handles leverage. Humans handle judgement."* (`BD-010`).

#### Section 7: Why `[STUDIO_NAME]`
* **Section Heading**: *"Engineered on principles, not marketing hype."*
* **7 Pillar Blocks**:
  1. *Problem-First Thinking* (Never selling premature solutions).
  2. *AI-Native Delivery* (Modern engineering speed with senior human oversight).
  3. *Senior Architectural Oversight* (Every blueprint signed off by experienced architects).
  4. *Transparent Estimation* (Confidence-banded budgets and timelines; no hidden surprises).
  5. *Zero Vendor Lock-In* (Clients own 100% of custom code, schemas, and IP).
  6. *Privacy by Design* (Enterprise zero-training API agreements; your data stays yours).
  7. *Long-Term Scalability* (Architectures designed to evolve into reusable assets and products).

#### Section 8: Trust, Integrity & Architectural Proof
* **Section Heading**: *"Built on verifiable engineering practices."*
* **Content Framework**:
  - *Engineering Rigor*: Modern Python ecosystem (FastAPI, Pydantic, SQLAlchemy).
  - *Process Transparency*: Exact demonstration of sprint cadence and weekly reviews.
  - *Privacy & Security Safeguards*: Strict zero-retention policies, PII scrubbers (`BD-014`).
  - *Note*: Explicitly avoids fabricated logos, placeholder client counts, or fake reviews (`BR-GDL-003`).

#### Section 9: Closing Conversion Anchor
* **Headline**: *"Have a business problem? Start there."*
* **Sub-headline**: *"Describe what's slowing your company down. We'll help translate it into the right technology, an actionable opportunity map, and an indicative estimate."*
* **Primary CTA**: **"Start With Your Problem"** (Button $\rightarrow$ `/discovery`).
* **Secondary Action**: *"Prefer direct conversation? [Talk to an Architect](/contact)"*.

### 7. Other Core MVP Page Specifications

### A. Services Index (`/services`) & Detail Pages (`/services/*`)
* **Page Objective**: Provide comprehensive technical and business depth on each of the 5 capability pillars (Software, AI, Automation, Integrations, Scale).
* **Key Content Sections**:
  1. *Pillar Mandate & Business Value*: Why this capability exists and what operational friction it solves.
  2. *Typical Challenges Addressed*: Specific scenarios (e.g. legacy ERP integration, slow database queries).
  3. *Technical Capabilities & Stack*: Python-first, asynchronous APIs, event queues, resilient database design.
  4. *Engineering Standards*: Automated tests, strict data contracts, observability, modular architecture.
  5. *In-Page Discovery Trigger*: *"Diagnose your needs in this domain: [Start With Your Problem](/discovery?pillar=[name])"*.

### B. Solutions Index (`/solutions`)
* **Page Objective**: Frame studio capabilities around specific, repeatable business outcomes rather than isolated technologies.
* **Key Blueprints Featured**:
  1. *Operational Workflow Consolidation* (Replacing multi-tool sprawl with unified software).
  2. *Intelligent Document & Data Processing* (LLM extraction pipelines for invoices, contracts, forms).
  3. *Legacy Software Refactoring* (Gradually strangling monolithic technical debt).
  4. *Startup MVP Acceleration* (Zero-to-one production builds with verified scalability).
* **CTA**: *"Have an outcome like this? Run a diagnostic."*

### C. How We Work (`/how-we-work`)
* **Page Objective**: De-risk the engagement model by making the 16-stage client lifecycle and the human-in-the-loop review process completely transparent.
* **Key Sections**:
  - Detailed breakdown of the 5-point North Star.
  - The Human-AI Collaboration Law (`BD-010`).
  - The Discovery-to-Delivery Roadmap (AI Diagnostic $\rightarrow$ Discovery Sprint $\rightarrow$ Production Sprints $\rightarrow$ Hypercare).
  - Code Ownership & Intellectual Property Rights Guarantee.

### D. About the Studio (`/about`)
* **Page Objective**: Humanize the studio, communicate the founding philosophy, and build intellectual credibility.
* **Key Sections**:
  - The Studio Origin & Problem We Saw (Generic agencies vs disconnected offshore freelancers).
  - Engineering Manifesto: Clean code, business alignment, durable assets.
  - India-First to Global Trajectory (`BD-003`).

### E. Contact / Advisory (`/contact`)
* **Page Objective**: Provide a direct, low-friction inquiry surface for enterprise prospects who prefer immediate human conversation over interactive diagnostics.
* **Form Fields (Data Minimized)**: Name, Corporate Email, Company Name, Operational Problem Description.
* **Operational Reassurance**: Clear notice of internal review turnaround policy (`BD-013`).

### F. Trust & Legal Pages (`/privacy`, `/terms`, `/security`)
* **Page Objective**: Satisfy all legal, ethical, and cybersecurity verification requirements.
* **Key Declarations**:
  - `/privacy`: Zero model training on client inputs, data minimization, cookie-free telemetry.
  - `/terms`: Commercial terms, IP assignment to clients, non-binding indicative estimate notice (`BD-006`).
  - `/security`: Threat modeling, SSL/TLS, API key rotation, credential sanitization.
