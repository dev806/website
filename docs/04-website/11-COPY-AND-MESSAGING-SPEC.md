## 1. Copywriting Philosophy & Tone Standards

Every sentence across `[STUDIO_NAME]` adheres to the **Voice & Tone Canon** (`DOC-BRD-005`): **intelligent, clear, confident, practical, premium, human, and technically credible**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                WHAT WE SAY VS. WHAT WE REJECT                          │
├───────────────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ WHAT WE SAY (APPROVED)                    │ WHAT WE STRICTLY REJECT (PROHIBITED)       │
├───────────────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ • "Have a business problem?               │ ✗ "Revolutionary AI 10x Transformation"    │
│   Let's turn it into technology."         │   (Unverifiable hype)                      │
```

### C. AI Discovery Stepper Copy & Mandatory Disclaimers

#### Stage 1: Problem Input
* **Heading**: *"What business problem are you facing?"*
* **Sub-heading**: *"Describe your operational bottleneck, manual work, or technology ambition in plain English. Don't worry about technical terms."*
* **Helper Text**: *"Recommended: 50 to 300 words. The more operational detail you share, the more accurate your opportunity map."*
* **Button**: **"Analyze Business Problem ➔"**

#### Stage 4: Opportunity Map
* **Heading**: *"Your Executive Opportunity Map"*
* **Sub-heading**: *"Here are the highest-leverage technology vectors synthesized from your operational inputs."*
* **Notice**: `100% Free & Ungated Strategic Synthesis`
* **Button**: **"Unlock Full Solution Blueprint & Indicative Estimate ➔"**

#### Stage 5: Progressive Gating Modal
* **Heading**: *"Where should we send your full blueprint?"*
* **Body Copy**: *"Enter your details to reveal the complete 18-section architectural blueprint and confidence-banded planning estimate."*
* **Consent Microcopy**: *"Send blueprint copy to my email and allow architect follow-up. We never spam, sell your information, or trigger automated marketing calls."*

#### Stage 6: Indicative Estimate & Mandatory Disclaimer (`BD-006`)
* **Heading**: *"Indicative Budget & Timeline Planning Bands"*
* **Mandatory Legal Disclaimer Copy**:
  > **IMPORTANT NOTICE:** This estimate is an automated, indicative planning range designed to help you gauge project scope and viability. It does NOT constitute a binding commercial quote or contractual proposal. Final scope, architecture, and Statements of Work (SOW) strictly require review and approval by a senior human architect.

#### Stage 7: Human Handoff Bridge
* **Heading**: *"Take the Next Step With a Senior Architect"*
* **Option A Button**: **"Request Human Architect Review (Free) ➔"**
  - Microcopy: *"A senior architect reviews this brief and emails an annotated evaluation within 1 business day."*
* **Option B Button**: **"Inquire About Paid Discovery Sprint ➔"**
  - Microcopy: *"A dedicated 1-week architectural engagement yielding production-ready specifications, credited toward build contracts."*
